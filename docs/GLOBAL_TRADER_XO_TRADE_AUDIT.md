# XO public trade-record audit

The [current scope](GLOBAL_XO_CURRENT_MARKET_SCOPE.md) applies XO's documented methods to fresh market setups. This audit preserves the original public evidence for those methods and management decisions. Strategy backtests and win-rate optimization remain stopped. The records do not supply private account access or a personal win rate.

Eleven original `@Trader_XO` post versions (ten canonical posts) have now been read directly, after secondary search results were used only to locate original IDs. The BTC edit history is also read. All 27 captured own-image references are checked: 22 unique byte contents have been visually inspected, and reused images/version attachments remain explicit. This remains a partial public-record audit, not a complete exchange-record or personal win-rate audit.

They do not yet reproduce event anchoring, the actual profile/value references, incremental exposure, conditional reversals or the exact published management. Preserve their results, and do not label them XO copy trades.
