# Global report charts

The clock below the chart shows current local date/time with seconds and the UTC offset. Candle axes, snapshot times and source times remain UTC. The latest quote captured for a report is separate from its completed daily/weekly and execution closes. The moving clock does not refresh saved analysis, move levels or reset zoom. PNGs show their saved snapshot time with seconds.

A primary downtrend can coexist with a conditional support-reclaim long; the selected setup heading follows the actual displayed plan. Read the dated structural reaction and subsequent entry condition rather than treating averages as automatic orders. Weekly plans retain macro context and weekly protection; the 15m report uses 5m execution evidence. New current-feed reports reject multi-day-old profile windows. Historical profiles in saved reports retain their original dates and do not establish current order flow.

Choose the instrument, report depth and timeframe, then read the assessment and its separate entry conditions. Each supported horizon provides a priced setup with Entry, SL and available objectives. SIDEWAYS setups retain range context and an explicit execution trigger.

New Pro reports open with broad price history containing an EMA ribbon, available anchored VWAP and deviation bands, volume and evidenced range references. Defaults are 120 candles for 15m/1H, 180 for 4H and 260 for daily/weekly, limited by available source data. Zoom changes the view; Full history shows all saved candles. Entry, protective SL and supported targets use the saved plan. Structure view provides the wider available studies. Older reports retain their available saved indicators.

Read the chart source and timestamps before comparing prices. Daily and weekly completed closes can be older than the entry study. A planned entry below the latest price is conditional: it does not predict a mandatory pullback. The chart does not connect the latest close to a remote entry as a required route. Target spacing does not predict arrival time.

The selector covers 15m Fast, 1H Intraday, 4H Swing, 1D Position and 1W Macro, and describes their setup mixes. Each horizon uses its own protection and objective context; the lower entry study refines execution. Lower-frame barriers remain visible without becoming main-frame targets. VWAP bands can provide objectives when the selected setup uses them; their anchor and timeframe matter. Range centers are geometric midpoints, not volume-profile points of control. Missing evidence stays unavailable. Chart indicators provide context alongside the setup; the trader makes the final decision.

Fresh setups apply documented trend, range, reclaim/retest and value-reversion methods to the selected market. An intact range and a breakout require different entries, protection and objectives. The explicit entry condition explains the reaction to observe at the saved price. Original trader publications provide source examples; a newly generated setup is a PULSE analysis of its own market snapshot.

Use Setup view, Value context, Structure view, Full history and individual study controls to inspect the saved evidence. Downloaded PNG charts use the same saved series and selected layers. Web, mobile and Telegram preserve the plan's prices. Chart titles identify the market and timeframe without opaque internal reference codes.

Zoom and pan remain in place through parent report refreshes, resizing, language changes, indicator switches and the Setup/Structure study presets within the same snapshot. Drag the chart to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom time, and drag the price axis to adjust price scale. **Reset view** restores the default time window and automatic price scale. **Reset studies** restores indicators without moving the viewport. Axis double-click does not reset the view. **Full history** and **Show measured swing** explicitly fit their requested history. Opening a different snapshot starts its own view. On mobile, use **Inspect candles** for gestures; the overview is a full saved map. PNG export is also a full report map using the selected layers, rather than a screenshot of temporary inspection zoom.

A conditional entry requires a new completed test after the snapshot. Earlier touches and completed moves do not establish that entry. Current targets are checked against both primary candles and newer execution candles, including wick touches. A recent primary extreme can be an objective before pivot confirmation; the displayed basis identifies it as an observed extreme. Nearby execution barriers remain separate from primary-horizon targets. Dates on target anchors describe their origin; they do not replace the report capture time or reference-candle close time. Free technical review corrects a saved snapshot from its retained candles without turning it into a live report.

Read each timeframe for its own market question:

| Chart | Context to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| 15m | Session value, session range and fast price reactions |
| 1H | Weekly value, intraday structure and nearby levels |
| 4H | Swing value, support/resistance and active balance |
| 1D | Multi-month recovery or decline, daily balance and position levels |
| 1W | Cycle value, major decline/recovery legs and weekly support/resistance |

The VWAP caption identifies its saved anchor and primary timeframe. Weekly bands cover the available cycle rather than a short execution window. Green/red zones come from primary-candle support/resistance evidence. Cycle, decline and recovery ranges describe their own observed legs; a current balance describes consolidation. A weekly view can retain both a major decline and the recovery that followed it. Their intermediate reference lines are geometric levels, not measured traded volume. Lower-timeframe entry studies do not replace daily or weekly context.

Fresh reports attempt to obtain an available complete exchange-trade window and reconcile it with the exchange candle. When that evidence is saved, **Volume profile** shows its traded-volume histogram and POC/VAL/VAH. **Trade footprint** shows actual taker sell/buy volume by price and completed candle. Read the venue, UTC dates and displayed price-row width in the caption: an archive profile can cover a shorter, earlier window than the primary chart. Adjacent display rows retain the full underlying volume. Saved profiles keep their original window when you zoom or review the report. Executed trades and resting order-book liquidity are separate evidence; a profile does not supply a historical order book.

Profile reference lines begin at their source window rather than extending across earlier macro history. A window newer than the last completed primary candle remains compact recent context. Panning before the profile window removes its overlay from that earlier view. The saved profile prices and volume remain unchanged.

Review current execution prices, protection and position size before signing any order. Spot SELL reduces owned inventory; research does not open leverage or activate Autopilot.

This public guide describes product use. Proprietary selection rules, parameter tuning, private research records and strategy source are excluded. Historical examples and software validation do not establish guaranteed trading performance.
