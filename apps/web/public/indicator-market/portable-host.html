Run the complete study over source bars

For v3 indicators, each input row supplies the actual executed buy and sell quantities for one source bar. The per-bar circuit uses that bar's quantity scale; the declared host pipeline then computes rolling values and conditions without looking ahead. A gap resets the calculation and its warm-up. If a captured edge bar is shorter than the declared bar width, it cannot count toward a rolling or cumulative window. A study without rolling transforms can show that captured reading with partial: true; it cannot confirm a crossover.

Numerical example only. These twenty contiguous rows are arithmetic examples with elapsed-millisecond timestamps. They are not market observations or a backtest. Replace them with your application's genuine source bars and source-coverage intervals.

Each row: {"ts":60000,"end":120000,"buy":3,"sell":1}. Quantities may be decimals in one consistent base-asset or contract unit. At most 1,024 ordered bars. Unknown source intervals must be supplied below; they are not filled with invented trades.
