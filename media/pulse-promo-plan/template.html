Editing notes for the selected chapter

Source times are inspected candidates. Fine trim points will be checked at the real click, scroll and confirmation during assembly. Target durations include deliberate reading holds and gentle reframing; they are not a promise to play every source range at its original speed.

Transitions, footage integrity and render checks
  • Match shared UI elements: Portfolio → funding control; report chart → Spot ticket; prediction scenario → strategy choice; journal → overview.
  • Use short eased dissolves and soft light transitions. Preserve market identity, action order and confirmed results. No abrupt glitch cuts or rapidly flashing emphasis.
  • The recorded WETH limit order is pending before owner cancellation. It is not presented as a filled buy. The running BTC Autopilot shows zero fills; the paused ETH vault has separate historical fills.
  • Gateway shows deposit approval and confirmation. Its Withdraw control is visible, but there is no completed withdrawal in these recordings. Risk Guard receives only the real landing-page introduction, not an invented workflow.
  • Cut idle loading, empty wallet panes and repeated scrolling. Preserve useful approval details and all states needed to understand the shown action.
  • Check every caption against the image below it, keep text and highlights off active controls, validate exact 240-second duration, review audio ducking and watch the complete final export.
